League format

How the schedule works

Rivalry rounds

Weeks 1 and 14 repeat the six fixed rivalry matchups, with the home venue reversed for the second meeting.

Friendship round

Week 12 repeats the six friendship matchups from their earlier regular-season meeting, again reversing the venue.

Standings round

Week 13 repeats pairings derived from the previous year's standings. Because 1 vs 2 was already a rivalry, the top four were adjusted to 1 vs 3 and 2 vs 4.

Home / away balance

Every team has exactly seven home and seven away games, and no team has more than two consecutive home or away games.
